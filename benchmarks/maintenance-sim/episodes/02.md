# Episode 02 — removal during a tick

Removal clears only future queued `remove` commands for that ID. A command
already extracted into the current tick batch still runs: a later same-batch
remove therefore yields `missing_entity`; a canceled future remove yields no
effect or error. A deferred spawn is never canceled and may reuse an ID.
Removal must never erase past emitted effects. Do not introduce a queued effect
store. Preserve all seed and Episode 01 behavior cumulatively; missing
prior behavior is a prerequisite failure, not an exemption.

## Public example

Input: `{"version":1,"operations":[{"op":"spawn","id":"a","x":0,"velocity":0},{"op":"enqueue","at":1,"sequence":1,"command":{"op":"remove","id":"a"}},{"op":"enqueue","at":1,"sequence":2,"command":{"op":"remove","id":"a"}},{"op":"tick","count":1}]}`

Output: `{"version":1,"state":{"tick":1,"entities":[]},"effects":["spawn:a","enqueue:1:1","enqueue:1:2","remove:a","tick:1"],"errors":["missing_entity"],"snapshots":[]}`
