## Controls that govern the next specification

1. **Define the decision-time information.** Freeze the initial packages, analyst materials, known change profile and candidate policy. Distinguish that profile from future details unavailable at adoption. Do not allow a selector to exploit per-run randomness or future patches. One starting-package decision is not free architecture switching at each episode.
2. **Use the established selection framework honestly.** Compare a frozen selector with development-selected and both fixed-package policies, size/API rules and an information/budget-matched ordinary review where the method-specific claim requires it. Do not claim novelty for these baselines or their arithmetic.
3. **Establish opportunity without cherry-picking.** A0 may test whether the available profile supports a plausible discriminating prediction. It cannot measure stochastic selection headroom without candidate data. Later all-profile results retain ties, uniformly good/bad tasks and adverse cases; never filter evaluation profiles using observed complementarity.
4. **Separate expectation from hindsight.** Compare expected outcomes conditional on allowed information, not the best realized package in each replicate. Noisy maxima inflate oracle estimates. Any estimated headroom is descriptive with uncertainty, not a deployable baseline; avoid normalized gap-closed scores when the denominator is near zero.
5. **Two holdouts and two kinds of variability.** A0 develops the method; distinct profiles test it. Final behavioral tests separately protect candidate scoring. Repeated implementation runs estimate executor variability, not repeated independent selector decisions. Either freeze one advice record per profile and limit the claim, or separately budget pre-outcome selector repetitions.
6. **Do not adjust away the mechanism.** Initial source size, declared API fit and local complexity may be prospective rivals. Actual changed files, failed builds, repair rounds and context traffic occur after assignment. Report them as outcomes/pathway evidence, not controls used to claim a purified causal architecture effect. Do not select only compiling/successful histories.
7. **Value is not free information.** Record one-time method development, package inspection, per-profile advice, executor and controller costs separately, with a realistic reuse count. A main fixed-executor study estimates choice quality, not end-to-end budget superiority. Claiming the latter needs a separately allocated credible alternative use of saved effort, such as ordinary verification or bounded repair.
8. **Retain policy and evidence boundaries.** No repair remains a restrictive diagnostic policy. A beneficiary-relevant repair policy requires a separate specification, development/holdout separation and full accounting; it is not silently introduced. Reference sentinels measure starting-state sensitivity, not protected repair or latent damage.
