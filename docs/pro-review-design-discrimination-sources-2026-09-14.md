## Decision and limits

This is a bounded methods review, not a fresh systematic review replacing the extensive [prior proposal ledger](architecture-maintenance-proposal-sources-2026-09-14.md) or [preceding review audit](pro-review-sources-2026-09-14.md). The decision is how to make the proposed architecture case discriminate prospective organization predictions from initial-generation, predecessor and feedback-policy effects, and whether a small A0 feasibility decision is justified.

The screen includes controlled source/predecessor interventions, inherited maintenance, scenario-based architecture analysis and directly relevant feedback/history controls. It separates target-program architecture from neural architecture, agent deployment and other-domain maintenance. A deferred work is not scientifically rejected. No new outcome or effect size for ALF is inferred from literature.
