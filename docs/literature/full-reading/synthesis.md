## Open extension and composition boundaries

[S115](S115-open-data-functions.md) establishes open constructor/function extension with a concrete advantage: later specific equations can outrank an existing default while recursive calls use the assembled function. Its tradeoffs are global meaning, duplicate-pattern conflicts, global exhaustiveness and a separate-compilation sketch needing additional constructor-interface support. Guard alternatives are unresolved. The paper demonstrates expressiveness, not a measured maintenance, runtime or interactive-development benefit. Selectable component composition and EML's modular conflict/exhaustiveness checks remain primary-method follow-ups; S118 and S116 retain pattern-view and practice roles. These distinctions narrow Nu/D1 transfer without discarding the mechanism's positive capability.

[S137](S137-data-types-a-la-carte.md) resolves selectable composition: signatures choose the actual constructor set, and modular algebras interpret it. It provides concrete reuse across differently assembled datatypes, with annotations, instance definitions, restricted injection search and richer-type encodings as costs. Free-monad descriptions also expose permitted operation categories under a chosen interpreter; they do not automatically restore external effects or serialize higher-order continuations. The original paper has no empirical cost/maintenance result. S138/S139 now have primary PDFs for checking the practical machinery and runtime tradeoffs that C02 already identified.

[S139](S139-composition-representation-cost.md) now supplies a measured representation-cost comparison. Generating a flat signature substantially improves scaling relative to linear and balanced datatype composition in one fixed-height expression-tree workload. The normalized plot retains roughly 1.3–1.5× plain-datatype time over much of its range; flat scaling does not establish zero overhead. Published snippets have count/index inconsistencies, and exact code/raw data were not located, so no runtime replication is claimed. Compilation, integration and maintenance costs remain unmeasured.
