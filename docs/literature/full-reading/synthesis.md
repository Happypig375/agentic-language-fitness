## Decision for human review

**Narrow the proposal and reuse established methods.** Retain a prospective, task-specific **package suitability** question, subject to A0 feasibility. Do not position chaining, inherited/reference controls, architectural maps, dependency planning, structured memory or history masking as new. The closest studies already implement them. The useful remaining decision is whether a maintainer could choose a package for a specified family of changes using predictions about responsibility and coordination that add value beyond initial correctness, source size and API availability.

The proposed next decision is whether to authorize **A0 only**: run both unchanged candidate baselines and construct at most two contrasting reference changes per package (two baselines, at most four successors). Before each witness, record its observable requirement, responsibility prediction, rival and disconfirmation. A0 must establish common behavior, credible alternatives and a nontrivial discriminator; it cannot establish an agent effect. Nu MMCC/ImSim remains one provisional same-language/engine pair, not a requirement. Reject or redirect it if physics/API differences, an observation seam that solves the task, or a broken baseline explain the entire contrast. A1, apparatus and live allocation remain separate decisions.

The strongest rival is **ordinary predecessor defects plus different API/size demands**, rather than an architectural effect. A falsifying observation is that the predicted responsibility advantage disappears after identifying the actual required engine/API work, or that the adverse change is equally localized in both packages. A future candidate result that disagrees with a frozen prediction is retained; an analyst cannot redefine the mechanism around the winner. A0 witnesses are feasibility work by the constructing analyst, not independent confirmation.

The current no-repair candidate policy can support only restrictive-policy robustness. For an inherited-state claim, retain predeclared reference-predecessor sentinels in both packages without selecting histories by success. A context/repair mechanism is **deferred**, including the earlier fixed-source history proposal; it needs its own targeted literature, treatment and allocation decision. No extra worker, map, helper, repair loop, task or model dispatch was added. New allocation is zero; 192+5 remains historical planning arithmetic, not an adopted sample.
