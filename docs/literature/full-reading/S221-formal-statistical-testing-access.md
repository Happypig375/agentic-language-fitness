## What the preview establishes

The abstract describes one C component from a nuclear emergency-shutdown system and **1,345 mutants**, introduced one at a time. Each of five statistical suites kills all of that reported set. Three formal suites also kill all; the other two leave one and two survivors. This is favorable fault-sensitivity evidence for both tested approaches within the selected mutation population. It is not a count of 1,345 naturally occurring industrial defects.

The introduction distinguishes tests derived from an algebraic specification and explicit test hypotheses from statistical tests whose distribution and length target a criterion. Page 274 begins the reachable-algebra/exhaustive-set account and ends mid-argument. ## Primary reconstruction recovered through the thesis

The author's publicly linked [1993 thesis, S222](S222-statistical-testing-thesis.md), Chapter III pp.78–80/Figure III.p, reports this same experiment with its control and observation choices. Five algebraic suites contain 282 inputs each. With closed-loop state, 122 of 1,467 generated mutants are classified equivalent, leaving **1,345**. Observing the calculated internal state as well as the filtered output leaves **0/0/1/2/0** survivors, matching the preview. Observing only the filtered output leaves **40/47/42/29/40**. The five structural-statistical suites contain 405 inputs, directly control internal state and leave no survivors among **1,416** eligible mutants after 51 equivalences.
