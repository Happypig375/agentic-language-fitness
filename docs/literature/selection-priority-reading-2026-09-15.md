## 3. How the local agent grows the work program

Maintain one live queue and claim-coverage matrix, extending the existing index rather than creating competing “final” lists. At each step:

1. Name the unresolved question and criterion: **U** (closest overlap/distinction), **V** (beneficiary, usable implication or cost), **S** (validity of a control, measurement, estimator or generalization). A paper can affect several.
2. Inspect relevant deferred entries in earlier ledgers and the current paper's bibliography and citing studies. Use targeted searches for competing explanations and replications as well as supportive work. Search new terminology when the old query demonstrably misses close methods.
3. Record DOI/title/version, discovery provenance, prior reading status and the decision it could change; deduplicate work lineages. Assign the next stable S ID for new entries without renumbering P/A/S records.
4. Acquire and read in full when resolving that question requires the body, appendices, figures, tables, equations or necessary supplements. An abstract may suffice to identify an unrelated topic, not to exclude a close study's uninspected methodology.
5. Reprioritize according to potential consequence, closeness, unresolved methodological detail and acquisition feasibility. A paper that could invalidate the main claim outranks another supportive example. Do not impose a top-five, top-twenty, two-page or citation-count quota.
6. After reconstruction, revise the affected claim/control and add any newly exposed dependency. A source that does not alter the plan can still close a documented uncertainty; redundant corroboration alone need not force indefinite expansion.

Scite searches request **20 records per page** and paginate at effective offsets beyond the first page when relevant records remain. This is a discovery page size, not a count of papers fully read or a maximum search depth. Record exact queries, examined ranges and why a query was exhausted, continued or reformulated. Citation graphs and “supporting/contrasting” labels guide primary reading, not votes for truth or proof of field coverage.

## 4. Reconstruction required for each promoted full read

Record the lawful PDF/supplement reference, hash, edition/date/notices and actual coverage. Read all pages and inspect the visual content needed to understand figures, tables, equations and image-based prompts. Report extraction/rendering limits. Acquisition, parsing, complete reading, method reconstruction and experimental reproduction are separate states.

Reconstruct **decision object and available information -> procedure/training -> comparators -> outcome generation -> scoring/uncertainty -> claim**, with source-page/section/table locators. Include actual independent units versus repeated runs, selection/exclusions, missingness, costs, valid alternatives, contrary observations and limits. Recalculate only published arithmetic when supported; do not invent missing data or call calculation a reproduction.

For conceptual or qualitative work, reconstruct its concepts, cases, reasoning and validation scope rather than invent experimental quantities. Separate the authors' reported evidence, their interpretation and ALF's proposed adaptation.

For each consequential ALF statement give one disposition: **retain with source**, **narrow/correct**, **withdraw/replace**, or **unresolved**, explaining how the source changes uniqueness, value or scientific validity. Amend the active proposal and PLAN when required; do not append summaries while leaving contradicted claims live. Preserve historical evidence and earlier versions.

## 5. Progress, access blocks and evidence-based handoff

Save after each paper: ID/DOI, edition/attachment/hash, status (`queued`, `acquired`, `partial`, `fully_read`, `reconstructed`, `synthesized`, `access_blocked`, or `deferred_with_reason`), last completed coverage, remaining method/artifact gaps, affected claims, new leads and next action. Reconcile these records with the existing index/assets/synthesis and commit coherent progress. Stages are not collapsed; a complete PDF can still leave an unreconstructable method.

**A batch boundary is a checkpoint, not a mandatory return to Chat.** Continue the next accessible consequential paper without requesting approval merely because S05, S07, S18, a page of search results or an initial batch is finished. An inaccessible PDF stays in the unresolved queue while independent accessible work continues. Existing subscription/access and repository permissions still apply; no circumvention, unapproved purchase or additional worker launch is permitted.

A scientific handoff is warranted when all three active criteria have a source-located, challenged disposition; the closest credible overlap and contrary mechanisms have been followed far enough for the claimed next decision; relevant query/citation follow-up has a recorded coverage rationale; and no known accessible unexamined lead could materially change that decision without being addressed or the claim explicitly narrowed. This is sufficiency for the declared decision, not proof that no other study exists.

If a material full text is unavailable, state the exact claim it blocks and whether the remaining plan is narrower or still unresolved. An unresolved novelty question stays unresolved; access denial cannot certify uniqueness. Genuine quota/context/budget exhaustion or required permission is a **partial checkpoint**, not scientific completion. Preserve exact DOI/version/page and executable next actions for a later session; ask for continuation only when it is actually required. No numerical reading quota is implied by resource-aware checkpointing.

The final synthesis must relate **U/V/S claims -> strongest supporting and challenging studies -> adopted or rejected controls -> residual uncertainty -> smallest next decision**. It may recommend retaining, narrowing, replicating, adapting or redirecting the proposal. Do not rescue firstness by changing the engine or expand the experiment simply because new methods were read. A0 and all experimental work still require their existing approvals.
