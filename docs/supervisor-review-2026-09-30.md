### S100/S101: retained state, replay, and correctness are different claims

The committed [S100 reconstruction](literature/full-reading/S100-live-model-checking.md) separates initial-state coverage from checking a reached state, finite safety evidence from infinite-path progress, and current values from outstanding historical obligations. The [S101 reconstruction](literature/full-reading/S101-operation-based-live-models.md) distinguishes operation replay from retaining a state whose old trace may no longer be reachable, and records manual read dependencies and integration effort.

These are useful synthesis results, not merely shortcomings to list. They yield distinct design choices: which state and history the developer wants to preserve; what dependencies/effects are represented; what independent property is checked; and what integration/runtime cost is shifted elsewhere. Their demonstrations are prior capability evidence even without a comparative user-benefit estimate. Absence of such an estimate is not a measured null.

For the broad survey, S108 is a concrete unresolved core-method dependency of S101. For D1, those papers motivate an honest temporal-observation contract but do not require graph merging, live migration or a model checker. Keep the intervention small enough to identify its intended contrast.

### Primary language semantics rechecked

Microsoft's [FS0025 explanation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/fsharp/language-reference/compiler-messages/fs0025) explicitly presents a complete enumeration and a wildcard as alternative ways to cover a missing pattern. Its [pattern reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/fsharp/language-reference/pattern-matching) specifies that a wildcard matches any input. Both pages were checked on 2026-09-30.

Thus D1's baseline can be behaviorally correct and exhaustive in both arms. Under a later type extension, enumeration can expose a missing site while a fallback silently supplies behavior. Whether that behavior is appropriate is an independent requirement. No compiler was run in this review, and the exact diagnostic/warning behavior of the future pinned toolchain still needs its authorized feasibility check.

This is a verification of narrow language semantics, not new academic discovery or a new full-reading assignment. No Scite search/report or Zotero write was performed or claimed in this pass.

## D1 feasibility amendments in operational terms

The amended proposal adds no arm and changes no authorized workload. It separates two maps needed for a future valid test:

1. **Source-site map:** original cases/payloads/guards, semantically valid fallback, targeted source difference, expected diagnostic opportunity under a controlled extension, and pinned warning policy.
2. **Behavior map:** old/new obligations, their triggers and observations, retained temporal work, and independent success/fault witnesses.

A site/obligation relationship can be many-to-many. A warning does not supply the intended new behavior. An exhaustive but incorrect branch must be rejectable by the oracle; a valid fallback must remain acceptable. Distinguish a diagnostic the probe can generate from one the candidate actually saw, and neither from unobservable attention. Keep the primary intention-to-treat comparison over all assigned attempts; do not adjust away failures or repairs that may be consequences of source assignment.

For temporal behavior, specify whether the requirement is a safety constraint, a bounded-response obligation, or something for which a finite trace is insufficient. Record event order, clocks, triggers, reset/external state and outstanding pre-checkpoint work when applicable. Exercise the monitor with positive/negative/vacuity cases before relying on it. Lack of a trigger is not automatic completion credit. A program violating required reachability is a failure, whereas an evaluator that did not establish the necessary observation has unknown coverage. A matching compiler diagnostic or a replay with no observed failure cannot fill that gap.

D1 still asks a locally useful, falsifiable practice question. Neither this clarification nor any predecessor's limitation confirms its uniqueness, expected effect, population value, source equivalence, or execution readiness.
